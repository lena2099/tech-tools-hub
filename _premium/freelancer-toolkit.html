The 2025 Freelancer Toolkit: Productivity & Platform Guide

A ready-to-use checklist, contract template, and rate calculator for remote work.

---

1. Productivity Checklist (Daily & Weekly)

Daily:
  • [ ] Block 3 hours for deep work (no notifications, no social media).
  • [ ] Use the Pomodoro Technique (25 min work, 5 min break) × 4.
  • [ ] Log all billable hours in a time tracker (e.g., Toggl, Clockify).
  • [ ] End the day by listing top 3 tasks for tomorrow.
    • Weekly:
  • [ ] Review your income vs. hours worked. Adjust rates if under $30/hr.
  • [ ] Clean your digital workspace: archive old files, close unused tabs.
  • [ ] Send one follow-up to a past client or warm lead.

    • ---

    2. Contract Template (Simple & Legally Sound)

    Scope of Work: “Freelancer will deliver [specific deliverable] by [date]. Client will provide [materials/feedback] within 48 hours.” Payment Terms:
  • Rate: $[amount] per hour / flat fee.
  • 50% upfront, 50% upon delivery.
  • Late fee: 5% after 15 days past due.
    • Revisions & Kill Fee:
  • Two rounds of revisions included. Additional changes billed at $[rate]/hr.
  • If project is canceled before completion, client pays for work done so far (kill fee: 25% of total).
Signature: “By replying to this email with ‘I agree,’ both parties accept these terms.”

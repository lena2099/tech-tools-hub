ADHD-Friendly Notion Productivity Dashboard for Remote Workers

A drag-and-drop dashboard designed to reduce overwhelm, build momentum, and keep you on track—without fighting your brain.

What’s Inside

  • Brain Dump – Capture every thought, task, or distraction instantly (one click to add).
  • Daily Focus Board – Limit to 3 tasks max (no 20-item lists). Each task has a timer toggle.
  • Energy Tracker – Log your energy level (1–5) every 2 hours. Spot your peak windows.
  • Body Double Timer – 25-minute focus sprints with a built-in break reminder.
  • Distraction Log – One-line capture for “what pulled me away” (no shame, just data).

    • ---

    Step-by-Step Setup (5 minutes)

    1. Duplicate the template into your Notion workspace. 2. Rename the dashboard to your name (e.g., “Alex’s Focus Hub”). 3. Drag your top 3 tasks from Brain Dump into Daily Focus Board each morning. 4. Set a 25-min timer using the Body Double Timer (or use a phone timer). 5. Log energy at 9am, 11am, 1pm, 3pm. Use the dropdown: 1 = exhausted, 5 = laser focus. 6. End of day: Move completed tasks to “Done” and delete empty distraction entries.

    Practical Example

    > Morning (9:15am): You feel scattered. Open the dashboard. Brain Dump has “reply to Sarah,” “fix login bug,” “order new mouse.” Drag only “fix login bug” into Daily Focus Board. Start Body Double Timer. Work 25 minutes. > After 25 min: Log energy = 3 (medium). Take a 5-min stretch. Start next sprint. > By noon: You’ve finished the bug. Move it to Done. Add “reply to Sarah” to Focus Board for afternoon.

    Pro Tips for ADHD Brains

  • Use the “Distraction Log” not as a guilt trap, but as a pattern finder. After 3 days, check: do you always get distracted at 3pm? Schedule a snack break then.
  • Color-code your Focus Board by effort level:
    • 🟢 Low effort (email, quick call) 🟡 Medium (report, design tweak) 🔴 High effort (deep work, coding, writing)
  • Set a weekly review alarm (Sunday 7pm) to archive old tasks and refresh your Brain Dump.

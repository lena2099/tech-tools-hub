The Prompts

1. Blog Post Outline

> "Create a detailed outline for a blog post titled '[Your Topic]' targeting [audience]. Include an intro, 5 main sections with subpoints, and a conclusion with a call to action."

2. Social Media Caption (Short)

> "Write a 150-character social media caption for [platform] promoting [product/service]. Add 3 relevant hashtags and a question to boost engagement."

3. Email Newsletter (Cold Outreach)

> "Draft a 4-sentence cold email to [recipient type] offering [value]. Keep it friendly, professional, and include a single clear CTA."

4. Product Description (E-commerce)

> "Write a 100-word product description for [product name]. Highlight 3 key benefits, use sensory language, and end with a compelling reason to buy."

5. Listicle (Top 5)

> "Generate a listicle titled 'Top 5 [Category] Tips for [Audience]'. For each tip, provide a one-sentence explanation and a short example."

6. Video Script (60 Seconds)

> "Write a 60-second video script for [platform] about [topic]. Include a hook (first 5 seconds), 3 key points, and a call to action."

7. FAQ Section

> "Create 5 frequently asked questions about [topic] with concise answers. Use a conversational tone and avoid jargon."

8. LinkedIn Post (Thought Leadership)

> "Write a 200-word LinkedIn post sharing a personal insight on [industry topic]. Start with a bold statement, add a short story, and end with a question."

9. Landing Page Headline + Subheadline

> "Generate 3 headline and subheadline pairs for a landing page promoting [offer]. Each pair should focus on a different benefit (speed, cost, quality)."

10. Content Repurposing (Blog to Twitter Thread)

> "Turn this blog post [paste URL or text] into a 5-tweet thread. Each tweet should be under 280 characters, with a clear takeaway and a hook for the next tweet."

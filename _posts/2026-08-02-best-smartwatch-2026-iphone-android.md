## FAQ

**Q: Can I use an Apple Watch with an Android phone?**
No. Apple Watch requires an iPhone. If you have Android, look at the Galaxy Watch 7 or the Garmin Venu 3, which are both excellent choices.

**Q: Which smartwatch has the best battery life in 2026?**
The Amazfit Balance leads with 14 days. The Garmin Venu 3 follows closely with 10 days. Most full-featured smartwatches (Apple/Samsung) top out at 1-2 days.

**Q: Do I need a cellular version of the smartwatch?**
Only if you want to leave your phone behind. The GPS + Bluetooth versions are cheaper and work fine for 95% of users. Cellular versions require a separate line from your carrier.

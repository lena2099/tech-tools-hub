## FAQ

**Is an air fryer worth it if I already have a convection oven?**

Yes, but only for speed. The air fryer preheats in 3 minutes vs 15 for my oven. For frozen foods, the air fryer produces better crisp because the fan is closer to the food. For actual baking or roasting a whole chicken, the oven wins.

**Can I put parchment paper in an air fryer?**

Yes, but weigh it down with food first. The fan will blow it into the heating element and it'll burn. I learned this the hard way — my kitchen smelled like a campfire for two days.

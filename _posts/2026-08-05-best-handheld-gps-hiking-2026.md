If I had to spend my own money today, I'd buy the Garmin eTrex SE. No question. It's the right mix of price, battery life, and simplicity for most hikers. I've owned three different Garmins over the years, and this is the one I keep grabbing.

The eTrex SE runs on two AA batteries, which is huge. I've been on trips where my buddy's fancy rechargeable unit died on day two, and he was stuck carrying a dead brick. I just swapped in fresh AAs and kept moving. The screen is small but sharp, and it's readable in direct sunlight. That matters more than you think. I've used it on day hikes and overnighters, and it's never let me down.

The one thing I hate? No onboard maps. You gotta load them through a computer first, which is annoying. It took me a solid hour to figure out the software. But once it's loaded, it just works. I've stopped caring about the setup because the payoff is worth it.

Now, if money were tight, I'd grab the Garmin eTrex 22x instead. It's older, the screen is low-res, and the buttons are stiff. But it's rugged as hell and does the same core job for less. I've dropped mine on rocks, left it in the rain, and it still fires up like it's brand new. I'd take that over a fragile touchscreen any day.

If I were doing serious backcountry, like multi-day solo treks, I'd spend more on the Garmin GPSMAP 67i. The inReach satellite messaging is a real lifesaver. I used it once from a ridge where my phone had zero signal, and my family got my location instantly. That peace of mind is worth the price. But it's heavy, the touchscreen sucks with wet fingers, and the subscription adds up. I would not buy the GPSMAP 66i though — it's the worst of both worlds. Older tech, same high price, and no real advantage over the 67i. I wouldn't touch it.

Honestly, I see people drop $600 on the 67i and then only use it for step tracking. That's a waste. If you're sticking to marked trails and weekend trips, you don't need the satellite stuff. You just need something reliable that won't die on you.

Seriously. Just get the eTrex SE. You'll thank me in 6 months.

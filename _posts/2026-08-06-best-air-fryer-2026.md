## FAQ

**Is the Ninja AF101 worth it over a $40 no-name air fryer?**

Yes, but not for the reasons you think. The cheap ones work fine for the first month, but they cook unevenly—one side burns while the other stays pale. The Ninja has a consistent heat distribution that I didn't appreciate until I cooked the same batch of wings in both. Also, the basket slides out smoothly, which matters more than you'd think.

**Can I put aluminum foil or parchment paper in an air fryer?**

Yes, but don't cover the bottom. The air needs to circulate underneath to crisp the food. I use parchment liners with holes in them—they're $8 for 100 on Amazon—and they save me from scrubbing the basket. Just don't let the paper fly up and touch the heating element. That's a fire hazard.

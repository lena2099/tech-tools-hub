## FAQ
### 1. How many watts do I need for a USB-C charging station?
For a phone and earbuds, 30–45W is enough. If you also charge a laptop, look for at least 65W, with 100W recommended for larger laptops. For multiple laptops, 200W is ideal.

### 2. Can I use a USB-C charging station with my iPhone or Android?
Yes, as long as your device supports USB-C Power Delivery (PD). iPhones (since iPhone 15) and most modern Android phones use USB-C, and they will charge at the maximum speed supported by both the charger and the device.

### 3. Is GaN technology worth it?
Absolutely. GaN (Gallium Nitride) chargers are smaller, cooler, and more efficient than older silicon-based chargers. They can deliver higher power in a compact size, making them perfect for travel and desk setups.

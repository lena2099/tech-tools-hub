## What Actually Matters (That Nobody Tells You)

**The DAC in your computer matters more than the speaker wire.** If your motherboard has a cheap audio chip, even $500 speakers will sound muddy. Most reviewers forget to mention this. If you're using a laptop's headphone jack, you're already losing.

**Size is physics.** A 3-inch driver can't produce real bass. It just can't. No amount of DSP trickery fixes that. If you listen to music with actual drums or play shooters where footsteps matter, you need at least a 4-inch woofer or a separate subwoofer.

**Bluetooth on desktop speakers is a trap.** The latency is fine for music but terrible for gaming. You'll hear your gunshot half a second after you click. Stick to wired connections for your PC.

## FAQ

**Do I need a subwoofer for desktop speakers?**

Only if you listen to bass-heavy music or play games where explosions matter. For most people, a good pair of 4-inch speakers is enough. You can always add a sub later if you feel something's missing.

**Are expensive speakers worth it for gaming?**

For competitive gaming, no. You need clear mids and highs for footsteps, and a $100 pair will do that. Spend the extra money on a good headset instead. Speakers are for immersion, not competitive advantage.

## FAQ

**Q: Is noise-cancelling under $100 actually effective?**

A: Yes, in 2026, budget ANC has matured significantly. Models like the Soundcore Space One can block out up to 90-95% of low-frequency noise (like engines and fans). They won't completely silence loud voices or high-pitched sounds like premium $300+ models, but they are highly effective for commuting and office use.

**Q: What is the difference between ANC and Passive Noise Isolation?**

A: Passive isolation is simply the physical blocking of sound by the ear cups. ANC (Active Noise Cancelling) uses microphones to pick up ambient noise and generate inverse sound waves to cancel it. Over-ear headphones under $100 generally have good passive isolation, and the ANC adds active cancellation on top. For best results, ensure you have a good seal with the ear pads.

**Q: Which is better for phone calls: the Soundcore Space One or the Sony WH-CH720N?**

A: The Soundcore Space One has a 6-microphone array that does a better job of separating your voice from background noise. The Sony is decent, but in windy conditions, the Soundcore handles voice pickup more clearly. For heavy call usage, the Space One is the winner.

Everyone says you need a robot that vacuums *and* mops. They're wrong. Most of these combo machines just drag a damp rag across your floor and call it a day. You're paying an extra $200 for a feature you'll use twice before forgetting it exists.

I ran four robots through my apartment for three weeks. I weighed the dust bins, timed the navigation, and read way too many Reddit threads about stuck rollers. Here's the thing nobody tells you: mopping is a bonus. What you actually need is a robot that vacuums hard and finds its way home without me having to crawl under the couch to rescue it.

## Best Overall: [Roborock Q5 Pro](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DWXBCQVP?tag=technolo0b423-20) ($299)

The Q5 Pro doesn't mop. That's fine. It's a vacuum first, and it's damn good at it. 5500Pa of suction picked up rice, cat litter, and a week of dust in one pass. The LiDAR navigation mapped my place in ten minutes and remembered it. No more watching a robot bounce off walls like a drunk Roomba.

Honest complaint: the dust bin is small. With two cats, I empty it every other day. Also, it struggles on thick rugs — I watched it spin its wheels on my bathroom mat for a solid minute before figuring it out. But on hard floors and tile, it's flawless. Buy this if you want reliable navigation and strong suction without paying flagship prices. Skip it if you actually need mopping — get the Dreame instead.

## Runner-Up / Better Value: [Dreame D10 Plus](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0D6TTRTPL?tag=technolo0b423-20) ($329)

The D10 Plus does everything the Roborock does, plus mopping, plus a self-empty base. For $30 more, you don't touch the dust bag for two months. That's real convenience. I ran it for two weeks and never opened the bin once. The mop handled dried coffee stains better than I expected, too.

Why it didn't win: the app is clunky. Setting up no-go zones took three tries, and the self-empty base sounds like a jet taking off for ten seconds every time it cycles. The mop pad also doesn't lift when it hits carpet, so I had to draw a no-go zone around my rug — annoying if you have mixed flooring. Buy this if you want a hands-off machine that empties itself. Skip it if you're on a budget — the Roborock gets you 90% of the cleaning for less.

## The Real Secret: [Levoit Core 300 Air Purifier](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07VVK39F7?tag=technolo0b423-20) ($89)

Here's the thing nobody talks about: your vacuum kicks up dust, and then you breathe it in. For $89, the Core 300 catches what my robot misses. I ran it next to the Q5 Pro for a week, and my allergies stopped acting up. This combo is the actual move — the vacuum gets the visible stuff, the Levoit gets the invisible stuff.

Honest complaint: the auto mode is dumb. It doesn't react fast enough to cooking smells, so I run it on manual high for 15 minutes after dinner. Filters cost $30 every six months, which is the real price of clean air. Buy this if you have allergies and want to actually breathe easier. Skip it if you think dust is a myth.

## The Overkill Option: [Dyson V15 Detect](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C2J8KJH9?tag=technolo0b423-20) ($749)

If you have stairs, or a car, or a couch you actually want to clean under — the Dyson V15 is the only one that makes sense. It's a handheld, not a robot, so it's a different tool entirely. But it's the best vacuum I've ever used. The laser dust detection is genuinely useful — it shows you the dust you can't see, and then you vacuum it up.

Honest complaint: it's heavy. I'm not weak, but after 15 minutes my arm gets tired. And the battery drains fast on max mode. But if you need a real vacuum for the places a robot can't reach, this is the one. Buy it if you have stairs or pets. Skip it if you only have flat floors — you're paying for power you don't need.

Stop buying robots based on the mop feature. Buy the one that vacuums hard, navigates without losing its mind, and fits your actual floor situation. The Q5 Pro is my pick for most people. If you want self-emptying, pay $30 more for the Dreame. And don't forget the Levoit — your lungs will thank you.

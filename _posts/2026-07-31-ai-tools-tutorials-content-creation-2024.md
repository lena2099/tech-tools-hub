## Beginner Friendly AI Video Generation Tools Step by Step

Video is the king of engagement, but many fear the complexity. These tools make it dead simple.

### Step 1: Choose a Tool

For absolute beginners, I recommend **Synthesia** or **Pictory**. Both require no video editing experience.

### Step 2: Write Your Script
Use an AI writing assistant (we'll cover those next) to generate a 60-second script. Keep it conversational.

### Step 3: Select an Avatar or Stock Footage
- Synthesia: Choose a realistic AI avatar that speaks your script.
- Pictory: Pick from millions of stock video clips.

### Step 4: Generate and Review
Hit "Generate". Most tools produce a draft in minutes. Review for timing and accuracy.

### Step 5: Export and Share
Export in 1080p or 4K. Upload directly to YouTube, Instagram, or TikTok.

**Pro Tip:** Use the same script for multiple videos by changing the avatar or background. Repurpose content effortlessly.

### Step-by-Step Tutorial: Write a Blog Post with ChatGPT

1. **Define your topic and audience** – e.g., "How to start a podcast for beginners"
2. **Generate an outline** – Prompt: "Create a 10-point outline for a blog post about starting a podcast. Include intro, gear list, recording tips, and promotion."
3. **Write section by section** – Ask for 200 words on each point.
4. **Edit and personalize** – Add your voice, examples, and anecdotes.
5. **Optimize for SEO** – Use a tool like **[Jasper AI](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C1N5P6QK?tag=technolo0b423-20)** (available on [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C1N5P6QK?tag=technolo0b423-20)) for advanced SEO features, tone adjustments, and plagiarism checks. Jasper integrates with Surfer SEO to optimize content in real-time.

**Note:** While free tools work, investing in a premium tool like Jasper AI (check it out [here](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C1N5P6QK?tag=technolo0b423-20)) gives you templates, brand voice consistency, and longer outputs.

### Step-by-Step Tutorial: Create a Social Media Graphic with Canva AI

1. **Open Canva** and create a new Instagram post (1080x1080).
2. **Use Magic Studio** – Click "Magic Media" and type a prompt: "Minimalist coffee cup on marble background, soft lighting."
3. **Edit with Magic Eraser** – Remove unwanted objects (e.g., a watermark).
4. **Add text and effects** – Use AI-powered text suggestions for captions.
5. **Resize for other platforms** – Click "Resize" and choose Facebook, Twitter, or LinkedIn.
